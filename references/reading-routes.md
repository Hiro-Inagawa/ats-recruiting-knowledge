# Reading routes

Choose the route for the decision and perspective, then read the indicated sections in their owning files. Chapter identifiers are stable across the ATS and LinkedIn manuals.

## Applicant and LinkedIn routes

| Route | Read in sequence | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Understanding recruiting | Chapters 1, 2, mechanism comparison, 5, 8, 10 | Explain records, actions, owners and uncertainty |
| Preparing an application | Chapters 1, 3, 4, 7; workflow A, then B if needed | Truthful role evidence and readable files |
| Screening and interviews | Chapters 5, 6, 7, 9; workflows C and F | Separate eligibility, evaluation and relationship context |
| Investigating a problem | Chapters 3, 8, 10; workflows B, D and E | Supported explanation, missing fact and next decision |
| LinkedIn discovery and evidence | Chapters 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16; workflows G, H and I | Truthful profile evidence, correct mechanism and bounded next decision |

## Recruiter routes

| Route | Sequence |
| --- | --- |
| Setting up recruitment | R1 requirements, R2 setup, R7 permissions, workflows J/K. Consult R9 before enabling assessment automation |
| Conducting recruitment | R3 intake, R4 screening, R5 interviews, R6 offers and handoff, workflows L–O. Read the connected case for the complete sequence |
| Investigating and improving recruitment | R7 exceptions, R8 reporting, R9 AI scrutiny, workflows P/Q. Read relevant underlying mechanisms in the existing manual |
