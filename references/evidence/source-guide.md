# Source guide

Source IDs identify locally readable original explanations of assessed supporting material. Read the explanation for a cited claim; external URLs provide attribution and a refresh route, not required reading. Product documentation establishes documented functionality, employer examples establish local practice, observations establish only the inspected sequence, and research depends on its method and scope. The manuals identify synthesis and hypothetical examples separately.

## Evidence and inference

A source capture is dated. Do not infer employer adoption, permissions or actual candidate outcomes from a documented feature. Do not treat a selected passage or frame as a complete source assessment. Maintain disagreements when the sources do not resolve them. A substantive uncertainty belongs with the recommendation that it constrains.

## Disagreements and transfers

- ATS11’s ambiguous AND sentence is reconciled with the consistent both-term semantics in ATS22; search and evaluation remain separate.
- LI12’s Lite Spotlights table disagrees with feature-specific LI04 and LI10. Exact account availability needs verification.
- ROP08 and ROP09 use overlapping time-to-hire terminology. Preserve the named report, event and candidate/opening unit rather than forcing a universal clock.
- ATS14 design practice and ROP13 engineering practice concern different responsibilities. Neither is an industry-wide rubric.
- ATS04 and DEMO02 retain differing page-date metadata. DEMO02 is sampled visual evidence; ATS05 is a separate publisher keynote transcript.
