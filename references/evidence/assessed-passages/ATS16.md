## Supporting explanation and applicability

Zander Brade’s 2019 Monzo account describes an application process in which answers were examined before the CV and portfolio. It contrasts with an assumption that every design employer starts with portfolio inspection. This is a historical practitioner account about one employer, not current Monzo instructions or a representative study. It supports asking what evidence a process examines and in what sequence, while keeping substantive work samples available for the stage that requires them.
