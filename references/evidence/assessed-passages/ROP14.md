## Supporting explanation and applicability

Raghavan, Barocas, Kleinberg and Levy examine public disclosures from 18 pre-employment assessment vendors. Their selected discussion explains choices about targets, data, representation and validation, alongside substantial limits: public disclosures cannot reveal how a specific model behaves in practice or everything a vendor does internally. This is a historical study, not a present benchmark of Ashby or LinkedIn. It supports asking for relevant validation evidence instead of treating marketing safeguards as proof of accuracy or fairness.
