## Supporting explanation and applicability

A public LinkedIn profile is a simplified view for signed-out users or external search contexts, with selectable eligible sections. It can differ from a signed-in member or Recruiter view. External search engines may refresh at their own pace after edits. Investigate the actual visibility context and current setting before diagnosing missing content. A delayed external result does not prove that the current member profile is wrong. The documentation does not establish a private employer’s access or certify broader privacy compliance.
