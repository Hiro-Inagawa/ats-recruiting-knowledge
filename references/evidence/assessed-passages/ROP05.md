## Supporting explanation and applicability

Greenhouse’s offer flow differs when approvals are enabled or absent. With the documented approval flow enabled, the offer must receive required approvals before extension. Without an applied flow, appropriately permitted users can perform the listed offer actions. Multiple approvers and multiple approval stages are different concepts. Inspect the actual job configuration and organizational authority before sending anything. Neither an offer record nor internal approval is candidate acceptance.
