## Supporting explanation and applicability

Workable's careers page builder publishes by default at apply.workable.com followed by the company's subdomain, which is usually the company name. Workable also documents a custom domain on certain plans, a widget placed on the employer's own site and a fully custom page built on its API. An apply.workable.com address identifies Workable hosting. An employer address or a careers subdomain does not exclude Workable. Treat the address as a dated observation of hosting and verify current documentation before relying on an exact format.
