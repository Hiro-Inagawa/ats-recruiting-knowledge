## Supporting explanation and applicability

GitLab’s scorecard guidance connects interview judgments with reasons and evidence available to the interviewer. Uncovered areas should not be filled with invented support. The handbook also records employer-specific budget and decision practices, including numeric thresholds; those are not general offer rules for other organizations. Use the example to make a judgment inspectable, not to manufacture a universal score. A disagreement requires examining what each interviewer observed and which criterion they were responsible for.

## Scope of the supporting material

Selected scorecard, justification, budget and assignment-model passages read. Numeric offer thresholds are employer-specific and not adopted as general advice.
