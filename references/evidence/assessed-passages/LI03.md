## Supporting explanation and applicability

Unified Search is an integration specification, requiring implemented Recruiter System Connect and customer enablement. It describes searching and actions such as importing a stub profile or sending InMail in the plugin. Enabling or disabling the customer integration affects résumé synchronization. A specification does not prove that a particular employer implemented it or that every field is current. Keep the LinkedIn member profile, synchronized data and employer application record separate; investigate the authoritative field and synchronization before correcting a stale copy.
