## Supporting explanation and applicability

In the documented Greenhouse flow, an authorized user records a candidate as hired and closes the associated opening. This is an ATS event. It does not by itself establish legal acceptance, employment commencement or a completed HR handoff. Keep approval, offer delivery, acceptance, hired recording and start date as separate facts. R6 and the connected case show the resulting records and why remaining active applications need their own disposition.
