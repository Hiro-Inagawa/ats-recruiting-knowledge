## Supporting explanation and applicability

Greenhouse’s evergreen guidance separates a requisition from its openings, records holds and reopening, and describes a new opening date after reopening. A report using the new opening can omit the earlier active interval. The guidance discusses both opening-to-hire and application-to-offer measures under overlapping terminology. ROP08 provides a more specific report definition. For comparison, state which clock includes a hold, whether a reopened opening is a new unit, and whether the original vacancy interval is also shown.
