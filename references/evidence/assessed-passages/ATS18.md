## Supporting explanation and applicability

Greenhouse’s data-quality guidance explains why dates, duplicated records and inconsistent process entries affect reporting. A report inherits the events and units recorded; a polished chart cannot repair an incomplete application history. Inspect record identity, stage use and timestamp completeness before comparing conversion or elapsed time. R8 and the connected case define cohorts and exclusions explicitly. A data-quality warning is a reason to investigate evidence, not proof that any specific recruiter decision was wrong.
