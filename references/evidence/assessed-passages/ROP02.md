## Supporting explanation and applicability

The documented one-stage job flow opens a job after its configured approvals and does not automatically reapprove later changes. The two-stage flow introduces a further gate for generating offers and restarts that stage for specified material changes. Multiple approvers can participate in either type; a stage is not a count of people. A recommended reassessment after changed requirements is governance synthesis, not an assertion that every configuration forces it. Recheck affected criteria, candidates and authority even when a software gate does not fire.
