# ATS25: Greenhouse: careers page integration options

Author/publisher: Greenhouse, corporate publisher.

Publication/update metadata: Updated 2026-03-02. Source context captured 2026-10-03.

Evidence type: Official product documentation.

Original source: [Greenhouse: careers page integration options](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/200721644-Choose-a-careers-page-integration-option).

## Supporting explanation and applicability

Greenhouse documents five ways to connect a careers page. With the hosted option the candidate sees boards.greenhouse.io followed by the employer's board path. With the embedded board and the API-driven options the candidate browses on the employer's own careers page. In one API-driven option the candidate browses on the employer's page and is sent to boards.greenhouse.io to apply, while the fully API-driven options keep both browsing and applying on the employer's page. A company careers address therefore does not establish that the employer does not use Greenhouse, and a boards.greenhouse.io address establishes hosting, not configuration. Treat the address as a dated observation of hosting and verify current documentation before relying on an exact format.

## Scope of the supporting material

Article read through a web extraction tool on the capture date. The five options and the candidate-facing address for each were reviewed. Setup times and difficulty labels are the publisher's own estimates.
