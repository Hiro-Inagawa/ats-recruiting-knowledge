Author/publisher: US Equal Employment Opportunity Commission.

## Supporting explanation and applicability

The EEOC guidance discusses applicants and workers with visual disabilities under the US ADA, including reasonable accommodation, confidentiality and concerns about assessment technology. Its relevance depends on covered jurisdiction, employer and circumstances. It supports identifying accommodation and sensitive-information questions before relying on an assessment, not manufacturing a global legal rule or collecting disability details unnecessarily. Verify current applicable guidance and involve the responsible HR or legal owner for an operational decision.
