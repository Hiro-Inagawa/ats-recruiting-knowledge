## Supporting explanation and applicability

Greenhouse describes configured rules driven by answers to custom application questions. Depending on the documented entitlement and setup, a matching answer can tag, advance or reject an application. The question is a prerequisite input, not itself an automatic action. A résumé term cannot be assumed to trigger this rule. For a suspected rule-based outcome, establish the question, answer, configured condition and action for that job; otherwise keep the cause unresolved. Tier and interface details need an account-specific current check.
