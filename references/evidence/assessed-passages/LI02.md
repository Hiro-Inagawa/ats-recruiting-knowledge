The Featured section lets a member curate posts, articles, documents and external work samples. The documented content is not discoverable through search and requires a signed-in LinkedIn viewer. Existing item visibility also matters. Featured therefore helps inspection of professional evidence after a profile is reached; it is not established as an independent keyword-filter channel.
