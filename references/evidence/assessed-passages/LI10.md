## Supporting explanation and applicability

Spotlights group candidates using activity, interests and relationships. Documented examples include company connections, company interest, prior interactions and ATS rediscovery when Recruiter System Connect is enabled. These signals are not direct evidence of job performance or guaranteed response. The source explicitly describes two Lite Spotlights, disagreeing with LI12’s comparison table. It also treats profile verification as one trust signal rather than a reason to disqualify an otherwise suitable applicant for lacking it.
