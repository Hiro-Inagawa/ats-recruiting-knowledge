Original source: [Practices to avoid](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a525117/).

## Supporting explanation and applicability

LinkedIn’s search-optimization guidance warns against repetitive keyword stuffing, misleading name-field additions and inflated experience or education. The recommended direction is accurate professional information. This supports improving truthful evidence rather than adding unsupported credentials or repeated phrases to chase visibility. It does not establish a specific keyword density, automatic penalty for all AI-written text, or a guaranteed hiring outcome. Distinguish a platform rule from an optional stylistic preference.
