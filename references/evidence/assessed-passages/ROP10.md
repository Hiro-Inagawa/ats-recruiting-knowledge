## Supporting explanation and applicability

US Office of Personnel Management guidance describes structured interviews using job-related questions and individually recorded ratings before group discussion. This provides an inspectable assessment approach: assign criteria, ask relevant questions and retain each observation before reconciling differences. It is government assessment guidance, not proof that any particular interview plan is valid or fair. Reasonable adjustments and role-specific evidence remain contextual responsibilities, and agreement after discussion is not a substitute for supporting evidence.
