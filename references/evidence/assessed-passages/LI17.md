## Supporting explanation and applicability

LinkedIn documents Boolean behavior for applicable Recruiter and Lite filters: inclusion options can mean any term or all terms, while exclusions remove matching terms. Typed Boolean strings and dropdown controls must be understood in their field context. Some fields differ, including Project filtering, and unsupported shorthand should not be assumed to behave like documented operators. A narrow query can exclude transferable candidates without showing they lack capability. Verify the current product and filter before transferring syntax from another ATS.
