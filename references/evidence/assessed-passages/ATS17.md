## Supporting explanation and applicability

Greenhouse documents that closing a job does not automatically resolve every active application. A vacancy’s closed label and an application’s state are separate records. If applicants remain active after closure, investigate the authorized disposition, alternative-role handling and communication rather than assuming each person was rejected or notified. R6 and the connected case show how an owner records closure and later resolves an active application. The product mechanism establishes possibility, not the cause of an individual applicant’s delay.

## Scope of the supporting material

Complete extracted article read. Active candidate records may remain active on a closed job; listed closure reasons are possible explanations, not a diagnosis.
