## Supporting explanation and applicability

Workable documents custom fields that can be used in filtering or reports, with visibility dependent on permissions. Those structured fields are not interchangeable with candidate free-text search. Investigate the field, filter and access scope if a record is not returned. A private field setting describes internal visibility in that product; it does not itself establish retention, disclosure or broader legal obligations. ATS22 supplies the contrasting text-search scope.
