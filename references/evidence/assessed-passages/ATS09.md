## Supporting explanation and applicability

Greenhouse documents predetermined scorecard attributes, recorded interviewer feedback and visibility affected by permissions. An employer must choose and configure the attributes. A scorecard can organize evidence but does not independently verify a rating or establish that all interviewers evaluated every criterion. Read the question, observation and assigned criterion behind a score. Treat unassessed attributes as uncovered, and distinguish access settings from broader privacy obligations. ROP04 adds the recruiter-operation explanation of the same mechanism.

## Scope of the supporting material

Predetermined attributes, feedback and permission-dependent visibility. Configuration does not establish actual employer rubric.
