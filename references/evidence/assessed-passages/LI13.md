## Supporting explanation and applicability

Open to Work supports choices about sharing career interests and opportunity preferences. Public sharing includes a visible frame, whereas recruiters-only sharing targets Recruiter users. LinkedIn describes steps to limit current-company visibility but expressly does not guarantee complete privacy. A preference can inform discovery without proving availability for every vacancy or suitability for a role. Confirm the person’s actual choices rather than assuming a frame, location or lack of signal establishes employment intent.
