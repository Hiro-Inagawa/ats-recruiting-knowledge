## Supporting explanation and applicability

Greenhouse scorecards use predetermined attributes and record interviewer judgments, with visibility conditioned by permissions. These records make evaluation inspectable but do not independently verify a rating. Separate observed behavior, the assigned criterion and the interviewer’s inference. An uncovered criterion needs evidence or a revised plan, not a fabricated score. Internal visibility restrictions do not settle broader access or disclosure obligations. ATS09 describes the applicant-facing interpretation of the same feature.
