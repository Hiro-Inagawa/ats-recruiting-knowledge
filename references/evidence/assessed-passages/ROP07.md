Original source: [Merge duplicate candidate profiles](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/115004506466-Merge-duplicate-candidate-profiles).

## Supporting explanation and applicability

Greenhouse flags potential duplicates according to configured matching conditions; a flag is not proof of identity. A merge is irreversible. Conflicting single-value fields generally use the primary record, while some multi-value information combines. The documented effects also include interview and integration identifiers that require attention. Confirm identity, separate applications, primary values and downstream dependencies before recommending an authorized merge. Do not erase legitimate multiple applications or infer that a LinkedIn URL alone resolves every conflict.
