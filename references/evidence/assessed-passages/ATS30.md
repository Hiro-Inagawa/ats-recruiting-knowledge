# ATS30: Greenhouse job board address observation

Author/publisher: Observation of public Greenhouse-hosted job pages.

Publication/update metadata: Observed 2026-10-03 with unauthenticated HTTP requests. Source context captured 2026-10-03.

Evidence type: Bounded public observation.

Original source: public job posts requested at boards.greenhouse.io and job-boards.greenhouse.io. Employers and postings are not named.

## Supporting explanation and applicability

A request to a job post at the documented boards.greenhouse.io address, in the form boards.greenhouse.io/<board token>/jobs/<job id>, was redirected to the same path on job-boards.greenhouse.io, which served a page titled as a job application for that posting. Two other current postings were reached directly at job-boards.greenhouse.io and served the same kind of application page. Greenhouse's support and developer documentation inspected on the same date names boards.greenhouse.io and does not mention job-boards.greenhouse.io. Both hosts are therefore treated as Greenhouse hosting, with the newer host supported by observation rather than documentation. The observation says nothing about any employer's configuration, and a future change of hosts would need a fresh check.

## Scope of the supporting material

Three public job pages requested once each on the observation date. Response status, final address and page title were recorded. Page content beyond the title, application handling and any other employer's pages were not inspected.
