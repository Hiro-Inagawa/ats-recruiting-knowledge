## Supporting explanation and applicability

LinkedIn’s résumé-sharing feature can use data from saved résumés for discovery when enabled. The documentation distinguishes that derived search data from handing every recruiter the complete résumé; applying or sending a document is a separate route to full access. It describes a historical résumé window and language eligibility, and warns that privacy cannot be guaranteed. Verify current settings and data before explaining a stale claim. Updating a profile does not automatically correct a previously submitted employer application.
