## Supporting explanation and applicability

Workable describes source labels attached to candidate records and conventions for recording application origins. Such a label represents the recorded route; it does not reconstruct every referral, conversation or influence that preceded the application. Preserve relevant provenance separately when investigating attribution. In a small cohort, a higher recorded hire share for one source does not show that source caused better hiring. The connected case illustrates the denominator and identity consequences of duplicated records.
