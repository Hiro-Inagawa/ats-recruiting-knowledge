## Supporting explanation and applicability

Greenhouse separates recording rejection from sending a rejection message. The documented workflow permits communication choices and delayed sending. A missing email therefore does not establish an active application, while a recorded rejection does not prove that a message was delivered. Investigate the application state, communication action and relevant timestamps separately. The example does not establish a universal waiting period or authorize contacting a recruiter without a user request.

## Scope of the supporting material

Complete extracted article read. Rejection and email choice/delay are distinct. Security-risk behavior not generalized to normal rejection.
