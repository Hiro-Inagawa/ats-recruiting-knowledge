## Supporting explanation and applicability

LinkedIn documents contextual, matched and similar skill features. Contextual evidence relates a skill to demonstrated work, while similar-skill discovery can surface adjacent experience. These feature descriptions support distinguishing evidence from a rigid exact-name-only rule. Claims about efficiency, confidence or finding overlooked talent are vendor positioning rather than independent outcome evaluation. Inspect the underlying work example before treating a skill association as direct experience.
