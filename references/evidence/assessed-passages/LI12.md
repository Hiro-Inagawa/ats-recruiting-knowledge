Product differences constrain what a recruiter may be able to inspect or do. The captured comparison table lists no Lite Spotlights, conflicting with LI04 and LI10’s feature-specific instructions. Do not silently pick one as a universal entitlement. Verify the current account and exact control before operational reliance; the product table is not a description of every employer’s process.
