## Supporting explanation and applicability

The Recruiter workplace-type filter uses shared Open to Work preferences for on-site, hybrid or remote work. Combining it with other filters can restrict results to candidates who have the relevant career-interest signal. This is a particular search mechanism, not a universal location rule for all LinkedIn searches. Inspect both preference and query conditions when reasoning about absence. The feature does not establish work authorization, willingness to accept a specific offer or candidate quality.
