## Supporting explanation and applicability

Flowserve’s public agency guide describes a tenant-specific submission sequence, including candidate completion after an agency initiates the record. Agency submission, candidate information and employer evaluation are different steps. This contrasts with direct applicant intake and helps explain why an agency-created record may still need candidate action. The seven-page extracted guide is a public process example from 2022, not current instructions for every Workday tenant, agency or applicant. Do not infer universal permission to create or modify an employer record.
