## Supporting explanation and applicability

GitLab’s published product-design job family describes responsibilities that vary by level, including design judgment, collaboration and AI-related work. Selected hiring-process passages describe interview and case preparation in that employer’s process. These are local expectations, not an industry-wide seniority rubric or evidence that a vacancy is currently open. Compare a design candidate’s decisions, scope and individual ownership with the target employer’s actual requirement. AI-related design judgment must not be silently replaced with an engineering requirement to deploy a model service. The supplementary role-level passages and original interview passages inform different parts of this comparison.

## Scope of the supporting material

Selected interview/process passages and design responsibilities across levels; linked employer rubrics not assessed.
