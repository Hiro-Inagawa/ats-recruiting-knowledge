## Supporting explanation and applicability

Greenhouse separates job approval from offer approval. Job controls regulate opening recruitment, while offer controls regulate extending a particular offer. A vacancy being open does not establish authority to send terms to a candidate. Identify which approval object, configured flow and owner apply. This documented distinction supports a clear requisition record and offer record; the organization’s broader budget or authority policy still needs confirmation.
