## Supporting explanation and applicability

Fabris and colleagues provide a multidisciplinary survey of algorithmic hiring, including systems, bias, measures, mitigation and governance. The assessed introduction presents benefits and harms as context-dependent questions rather than resolving a universal superiority claim. The selected later discussion explains that changes in the surrounding hiring context can make earlier accuracy or fairness evaluations obsolete, with fresh evidence complicated by delayed feedback and sensitive data. This supports monitoring context and uncertainty; it does not measure a current vendor or guarantee that a human reviewer eliminates bias.
