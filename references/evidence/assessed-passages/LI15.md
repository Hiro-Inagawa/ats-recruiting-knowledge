## Supporting explanation and applicability

LinkedIn explains that ordinary people-search relevance varies with the searcher, query and context. A small set of test accounts is therefore not representative of all search positions. The page recommends accurate keywords and warns that excessive optimization may interact with spam detection; it does not disclose fixed ranking weights or a safe density threshold. Keep ordinary member search separate from Recruiter filter behavior. Views or appearances can describe visibility but do not by themselves show a profile edit caused more suitable hiring conversations.
