## Supporting explanation and applicability

The documented application-review view exposes candidate information, attachments and answers, with actions for progression or rejection. Prior applications may be inspectable subject to access. These are distinct records: an attachment is not proof that parsing succeeded, and an available review action is not evidence that a recruiter used it. To investigate a particular application, identify the job, the application record, the visible evidence and the user’s permissions before attributing an outcome.

## Scope of the supporting material

Review actions, attachments, question responses and prior application access; employer permissions and actual use remain unknown.
