# ATS26: Greenhouse: hosted job board and job post URLs

Author/publisher: Greenhouse, corporate publisher.

Publication/update metadata: Updated 2026-03-02. Source context captured 2026-10-03.

Evidence type: Official product documentation.

Original source: [Greenhouse: hosted job board and job post URLs](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/360020561392-Job-post-URL-for-Greenhouse-hosted-job-posts).

## Supporting explanation and applicability

A Greenhouse-hosted job board address combines the fixed root boards.greenhouse.io with an editable job board token, such as a company name. A hosted job post link combines that root with a gh_jid parameter carrying the job's identifier. The companion article on the job board URL describes the token and its editing by a site administrator. Both describe Greenhouse hosting and say nothing about an employer's stages, rules or reviewers. Treat the address as a dated observation of hosting and verify current documentation before relying on an exact format.
