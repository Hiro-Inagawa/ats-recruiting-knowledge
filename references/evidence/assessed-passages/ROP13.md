## Supporting explanation and applicability

GitLab’s machine-learning engineering job family describes production-oriented technical responsibilities and varying levels of ownership. This is an employer-specific engineering example that contrasts with the product-design expectations in ATS14. A designer’s prototype or interaction-design contribution does not establish engineering deployment, monitoring or service ownership. Conversely, lacking model-operation duties need not disqualify a designer when those duties are not essential to the target role. Compare the actual responsibility, not merely the presence of the word AI.
