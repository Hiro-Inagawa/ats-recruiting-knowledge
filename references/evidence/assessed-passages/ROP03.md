## Supporting explanation and applicability

Greenhouse interview plans organize stages, interviews and evaluation focus. Consistent naming matters for report interpretation. A stage is a process location, while an interview and its assigned criteria produce evaluative evidence. Design the sequence around the work requirement and record who covers each criterion. The documented structure does not establish that a specific plan causes stronger hires; inspect actual evidence coverage and process delays rather than treating a configured plan as proof of quality.
