# Recruiter workflows J–Q

Read the relevant subject in [Recruiter operations](../manuals/recruiter-operations.md) and the [connected case](../examples/recruiting-case-and-metrics.md). AI scrutiny belongs inside the decision, not as a substitute for evidence.

### J. Requisition and requirement review

**Use when:** a hiring need is proposed or materially changed. **Inputs:** work outcomes, level, essential capabilities, support, opening/budget boundary, constraints and decision owners. **Owner:** manager with recruiter and approver.

Map each criterion to work and observable evidence. Separate entry requirements from preferences. Choose approve, revise or pause. Record version, authorization, reasons, unresolved facts and next owner. If changed, identify affected candidates, job text, rules and assessments. Escalate terms, eligibility or authority questions to their owner. **Result:** versioned requisition decision, not an inferred software approval. **Example:** PD-AI-01-v1 approval in R1, followed by v2 change review in the case. **Limit:** actual budget/legal authority must come from the employer.

### K. Job and process setup

**Use when:** preparing intake or revising stages. **Inputs:** approved criteria/version, role owners, evidence formats, interview plan, communication/access requirements and proposed rules. **Owner:** recruiter with manager, administrator and relevant specialists.

Assign a purpose, evidence opportunity and assessor to each stage. Review scorecard coverage and candidate burden. Review questions separately from automated actions and establish exception handling. Confirm adjustment contact and permissions. **Result:** ready/not-ready setup record with missing approvals and owner. **Example:** R2 replaces a redundant conversation with a failure-state discussion. **Limit:** exact interface, entitlement and rule behavior require current tenant verification. No candidate-facing publication is performed by this workflow alone.

### L. Sourcing and intake

**Use when:** a person enters through direct, referral, agency or prior-applicant routes. **Inputs:** opening, provenance, application status, permitted evidence, relationship history and access conditions. **Owner:** recruiter, with agency/client owner where relevant.

Separate person identity from application. Record introduction and completion events, source convention, requested evidence and next permitted contact. Investigate possible duplicates through P. **Result:** intake record with completeness, provenance and responsible next step. **Example:** R3 preserves A-OLD while establishing A-01. **Decision:** proceed when the authorized application/evidence is sufficient for screening, clarify missing completion, or hold unresolved representation/access. **Limit:** a public profile or referral is not proof of qualification or blanket data-use permission.

### M. Screening and shortlisting

**Use when:** deciding the next assessment step. **Inputs:** application, approved criteria version, actual material and any configured rule/AI rationale. **Owner:** recruiter or assigned reviewer.

Map evidence to demonstrated, transferable, unestablished or contradicted capability. Check decisive missing facts and actual eligibility conditions. Review generated conclusions against source material. **Result:** advance/clarify/hold/decline record with reason, communication owner and next step. **Example:** R4 advances design evidence while leaving infrastructure ownership unestablished. **Escalate:** unapproved criteria, sensitive information or unresolved eligibility. **Limit:** no universal score and no decision inferred from timing alone.

### N. Interview and debrief

**Use when:** gathering or reconciling capability evidence. **Inputs:** assigned criterion, comparable questions, actual notes/material, adjustments and individual scorecards. **Owner:** interviewers, with manager responsible for the debrief recommendation.

Preserve observation separately from interpretation. Mark unassessed criteria. Compare conflicting judgments by evidence and criterion version. Obtain focused clarification where a decisive gap remains. **Result:** evidence-linked evaluation plus debrief decision, remaining risks/support and next owner. **Example:** R5 resolves design versus deployment ownership. **Escalate:** unresolved essential capability or an inappropriate assessment condition. **Limit:** group agreement and averaging do not create missing evidence.

### O. Offer, handoff and closure

**Use when:** a selection recommendation is made, an offer changes, a candidate withdraws or an opening closes. **Inputs:** recommendation, authorized terms/version, approval, delivery/acceptance evidence, conditions, application statuses and handoff owner. **Owner:** recruiter, approver and HR operations.

Track each event separately. Review revised terms for reapproval. Reconcile withdrawal/application scope. Hand off necessary information and confirm receipt. Review remaining active applications and communication. **Result:** offer/handoff/closure record with outstanding actions, not just a closed job label. **Example:** R6 preserves distinct dates and A-04's communication. **Limit:** ATS state does not establish legal acceptance or actual start.

### P. Record exception investigation

**Use when:** duplicates, conflicting facts, hidden fields or stale integrations affect a decision. **Inputs:** record identities, conflicting values, source/version/time, permissions, dependencies and correction request. **Owner:** recruiter with authorized administrator/integration/privacy owner.

Preserve the discrepancy. Check identity, application context and authority for the specific fact. Determine merge/correction consequences before any separately authorized action. Check downstream agreement after a permitted correction. **Result:** discrepancy record, decision to hold/correct/escalate and readback requirements. **Example:** R7 holds an irreversible merge pending identity/integration review. **Limit:** visibility is not accuracy, and potential matching does not authorize a merge.

### Q. Reporting and process review

**Use when:** interpreting performance or investigating a bottleneck. **Inputs:** metric question, unit/cohort/cutoff, event data, criteria/stage versions, duplicates, withdrawals, holds and missingness. **Owner:** recruiter/operations analyst with process owner.

Define the calculation before interpreting it. Reconcile distinct applications/openings. Compute counts and labeled clocks. Show sensitivity to exclusions and reopening. Identify the specific next investigation, not an invented cause. **Result:** reproducible report record with definitions, missingness, conclusion and limits. **Example:** R8 and the connected case calculate stage conversions and alternate clocks. **Escalate:** unreliable events or undocumented report implementation. **Limit:** small counts and correlations do not establish channel effectiveness or causal process improvements.
