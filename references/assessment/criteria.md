# Assessment criteria

These criteria are an original assessment synthesis of the owning knowledge. They organize inspection, not an employer score or universal hiring rubric. A positive result concerns the inspected evidence and purpose. It does not establish an employer outcome. Read the owning passages and their evidence when applying a criterion.

The executable catalogue contains the same thresholds and examples. Stable criterion IDs identify checks across revisions.

## shared.positioning

**Professional positioning.** Can the reader identify the kind of work and understand the intended professional direction?

Applies to the résumé and the LinkedIn profile in every mode. Necessary input categories: content. A context-based non-applicability decision is not a substitute for inspecting this foundation.

**Adequate evidence:** A description connected to actual roles and relevant work lets the reader identify the professional contribution. More than one specialty is compatible with clear positioning.

**Finding threshold:** Descriptions that conflict with the inspected experience, or obscure the work relevant to the stated purpose, support a bounded clarification. A preference for a narrower identity does not.

| Case | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Adequate | Product Designer supported by interaction design and systems work. |
| Problematic | An unsupported executive title contradicts the recorded role. |
| Uncertain | The target is not supplied, so specialization for a specific vacancy remains conditional. |

This threshold is original synthesis. Its owning explanation is [Chapter 7: Interviews, scorecards and design evidence](../manuals/ats-and-recruiting.md). Product documentation and employer examples within that explanation retain their distinct evidence roles.

## shared.relevance

**Relevant experience.** Can relevant experience be located and connected to the stated purpose?

Applies to the résumé and the LinkedIn profile in every mode. Necessary input categories: content. A context-based non-applicability decision is not a substitute for inspecting this foundation.

**Adequate evidence:** Relevant responsibilities or examples are identifiable. General assessment can establish what is demonstrated without inventing a target or interpreting omitted work as inability.

**Finding threshold:** Important supplied evidence is difficult to locate, or the document claims a requirement is met when the inspected evidence contradicts it. Unrelated experience is not inherently harmful.

| Case | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Adequate | A systems role points to component decisions and governance. |
| Problematic | An asserted essential qualification conflicts with the supplied record. |
| Uncertain | A relevant capability is not described and must be clarified rather than declared absent. |

## shared.chronology

**Chronology and factual consistency.** Are dates, employers, roles and project chronology understandable and internally compatible?

Applies to the résumé and the LinkedIn profile in every mode. Necessary input categories: content. A context-based non-applicability decision is not a substitute for inspecting this foundation.

**Adequate evidence:** The inspected chronology is understandable and has no demonstrated consequential contradiction. Overlapping consulting and employment can be compatible.

**Finding threshold:** An actual contradictory date, employer or sequence warrants correction. A gap, overlap or older role alone does not establish a defect.

| Case | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Adequate | Employment and a concurrent independent project are labeled distinctly. |
| Problematic | The same role has incompatible start dates in supplied passages. |
| Uncertain | A project date is not supplied, so its relationship to a later launch is unresolved. |

This threshold is original synthesis. Its owning explanation is [Chapter 2: Candidate intake and records](../manuals/ats-and-recruiting.md). Product documentation and employer examples within that explanation retain their distinct evidence roles.

## shared.contribution

**Individual contribution.** Can individual responsibility be distinguished from the work of a team?

Applies to the résumé and the LinkedIn profile in every mode. Necessary input categories: content. A context-based non-applicability decision is allowed with an evidenced reason.

**Adequate evidence:** The text identifies personal decisions, responsibilities or outputs in their team context. Collaboration does not require claiming sole ownership.

**Finding threshold:** A consequential ownership claim contradicts the inspected material, or unclear contribution prevents evaluating relevant responsibility. Missing ownership evidence is a clarification.

| Case | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Adequate | Owned interaction design while engineers deployed the service. |
| Problematic | Claims sole implementation while the supplied case assigns implementation elsewhere. |
| Uncertain | A team launch is described without identifying the candidate contribution. |

This threshold is original synthesis. Its owning explanation is [R5. Interviews and evaluation](../manuals/recruiter-operations.md). Product documentation and employer examples within that explanation retain their distinct evidence roles.

## shared.claims

**Supported claims and outcomes.** Do consequential claims stay within the scope of their supporting evidence?

Applies to the résumé and the LinkedIn profile in every mode. Necessary input categories: content. A context-based non-applicability decision is allowed with an evidenced reason.

**Adequate evidence:** Claims are compatible with the available evidence and distinguish prototypes from production, observed results from forecasts, and contribution from unsupported causality.

**Finding threshold:** A demonstrated contradiction is material. Missing measurement or deployment evidence supports a clarification, not an accusation of dishonesty.

| Case | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Adequate | Evaluated a prototype with a stated test scope. |
| Problematic | Calls the same five-user prototype an established production deployment without reconciling phases. |
| Uncertain | A causal business outcome is asserted but the evidence establishes only participation. |

## shared.proof

## resume.extraction

**Text extraction and reading order.** Does an inspected original file produce the important text in an understandable order?

Applies to the résumé in every mode. Necessary input categories: content, original, extraction. A context-based non-applicability decision is not a substitute for inspecting this foundation.

**Adequate evidence:** An observation derived from the supplied original establishes relevant content and reading order. A local extraction is evidence about that file, not every ATS.

**Finding threshold:** Demonstrated missing or scrambled important text supports a correction. Silence or a quick rejection does not establish a parsing failure.

| Case | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Adequate | The supplied PDF extraction preserves names, roles and bullets in order. |
| Problematic | An inspected extraction omits the contact details visible in the original. |
| Uncertain | Only pasted text is supplied, so original-file extraction cannot be checked. |

This threshold is original synthesis. Its owning explanation is [Chapter 3: Parsing and structured information](../manuals/ats-and-recruiting.md). Product documentation and employer examples within that explanation retain their distinct evidence roles.

## resume.fields

**Sections and employment information.** Are the important sections, employers, titles and dates identifiable?

Applies to the résumé in every mode. Necessary input categories: content. A context-based non-applicability decision is not a substitute for inspecting this foundation.

**Adequate evidence:** A reader can associate employment information with its role and inspect relevant sections. Conventional headings can help without imposing a universal template.

**Finding threshold:** Ambiguous association or demonstrated missing necessary information supports a bounded change. An unconventional but understandable arrangement is not a defect. Type size, density and layout are presentation choices that support at most an optional note unless the inspected original loses or misassociates text.

| Case | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Adequate | Each role has an identifiable employer and dates. |
| Problematic | A date cannot be associated with either of two roles. |
| Uncertain | An excerpt excludes the employment section, so completeness is unestablished. |

This threshold is original synthesis. Its owning explanation is [Chapter 3: Parsing and structured information](../manuals/ats-and-recruiting.md). Product documentation and employer examples within that explanation retain their distinct evidence roles.

## target.requirements

**Required and preferred criteria.** Are target requirements distinguished from preferences and assessed against their actual wording?

Applies to the résumé and the LinkedIn profile in target-role mode. Necessary input categories: content, target. A context-based non-applicability decision is not a substitute for inspecting this foundation.

**Adequate evidence:** The supplied target identifies relevant requirements and the comparison respects their conditions. Employer clarification is needed where wording is ambiguous.

**Finding threshold:** Treating a preference as mandatory or declaring eligibility without evidence supports correction or clarification.

| --- | --- |
| Adequate | Deployment ownership is preferred and interaction design is required. |
| Problematic | Declares the applicant ineligible solely for a preferred tool. |
| Uncertain | No vacancy text or sufficiently defined target is supplied. |

## consistency.facts

**Dates and titles across assets.** Are consequential dates, employers and titles compatible across supplied versions?

Applies across the supplied assets in consistency mode. Necessary input categories: comparison. A context-based non-applicability decision is not a substitute for inspecting this foundation.

**Adequate evidence:** Differences are compatible or explained by version, official title or audience context. Exact wording need not be identical.

**Finding threshold:** Inspected incompatible facts justify correction after chronology and version are checked.

| Case | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Adequate | A functional headline differs from the accurate official employment title. |
| Problematic | The same role has conflicting end dates in current supplied assets. |

Applies across the supplied assets in consistency mode. Necessary input categories: comparison. A context-based non-applicability decision is not a substitute for inspecting this foundation.

**Adequate evidence:** Current and historical records are distinguished. Different emphasis for different audiences is allowed without assuming synchronization.

**Finding threshold:** A stale current record or material contradiction needs a bounded response. Do not edit historical submissions to erase their evidence.
